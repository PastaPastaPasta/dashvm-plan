**Scope decision on replacement.** The operator note asks for "the authorized replacement,
cancellation and top-up transitions". Replacement is not given its own transition here. A
replacement is a contract update that carries a new executable version while a round is current
(the DIP: "Only authorized update actions change a pending candidate. Replacing it atomically
cancels the old round and its timer"), and the transition that carries executable code is
R06-05 part 2 / R06-04's (the contract create/update converter emits
`ReadinessOperationType::OpenRound`, per R08-12's plan 3.7). A separate `Replace` action carrying a
bundle digest with no code behind it would open rounds for bundles nothing validated, and would
be deleted the day the update converter lands. What this task delivers for Q12 is the rule and its
proof: the Drive primitive R08-12 ships performs the replacement atomically, and the tests here
drive it through a block (the primitive applied in a block's transaction where the converter will
sit) to pin that the old round's reports, cursor, fund and deadline never transfer, that the old
payer is refunded, that exactly one pending update exists per contract, and that the previous
active version is untouched. Stated in the PR body and in section 3.4.

## 1. Acceptance in my words

**Q13 (cancellation, refunds, no expiry).** A contract's owner may cancel the contract's pending
executable version at any point before activation, including during the additional wait after the
80 percent crossing, with a signed, paid state transition bound to the exact pending round. The
cancellation retires the round in one batch: the pointer is deleted, the round is queued for the
bounded cleanup, its deadline entry (and an emptied time bucket) is removed, its fund is emptied,
the cleanup reserve is charged to the epoch processing pool for the deferred cleanup that already
happened or will happen, and the remainder is credited to the **recorded payer** of the round, not
to the signer. Nothing expires on its own: a round with too few reports, or a fund-short round,
stays pending for as long as the chain runs, costing at most two reads per block visit, until the
owner cancels it, a contract update replaces it, or it crosses and activates. A recorded payer, or
the owner, may add credits to a pending round's fund (a top-up) so that a fund-short verification
walk can continue; whatever the walk does not spend returns to the recorded payer when the round
retires. Every accepted manifest and canonical module blob is permanent: cancellation and
replacement write only under `[112, r]`, `[40, 129]`, identity balances and the epoch pool, so
they cannot delete code wherever the archive lives (R06-04's EXEC namespace).

**Q12 (one pending update, atomic replacement, votes bound to the round).** A contract has at most
one current round (the pointer `c`). Opening a round for a contract that already has one retires
the old round in the same batch (pointer swap, retirement, fund settlement, refund netting), so
there is never a moment with two pending versions and never a moment with none between them. The
round id includes the accepted height, the bundle digest and the version; reports live under the
round's key; a report for the old round id is not a report for the new round, the new round
starts at raw count zero, the old cursor and mark are unreachable, and the old deadline entry is
gone. The current active version (R08-13's activation record) is never touched by a replacement or
a cancellation: current code serves until the replacement is ready.

**Q10 (not broken).** Cancellation and top-up never touch the count tree, the scan cursor or the
evaluation mark; a top-up does not clear `awaiting_funding` (the walk owns that flag and resumes
on the next visit because the mark is unwritten); a cancelled round is unreachable through the
pointer so the walk never visits it again.

**Recovery without a below-threshold fallback (consensus half).** Time alone never activates: a
round at 79.9 percent of the agreed set stays pending for years of block time, a round with zero
reports stays pending, and a node restarted between blocks resumes from Drive state with the same
round, mark, cursor and fund. The node-local half (a node without a compatible compiled artifact is
locally unready, never rejects a consensus-valid call, and prepares from the permanent canonical
state and the activation record after a restart, cache loss or engine upgrade) is R08-06's and
FIX-09's and is named in the PR body, not implemented here.

**Named-module scope.** Rounds bind the whole bundle digest; there is no per-module round, so
cancellation and replacement are whole-bundle by construction.

```rust

pub struct ContractPendingVersionTransitionV0 {

    pub data_contract_id: Identifier,         // the contract whose pending version is acted on

/// serde `tag = "$type", rename_all = "camelCase"` like `ContractUserModerationAction`
pub enum ContractPendingVersionAction {

    Cancel,
    /// Adds credits to the pending version's readiness fund.
    TopUp { amount: Credits },
```

     `fetch_readiness_round_operations` plus `Drive::calculate_fee` over the read operations, the
     shape of `fetch_contract_fee_pot_with_fee`); fee added; `None` is
     `ContractPendingVersionNotFoundError`.
  3. `round.round_id() != self.round_id()` is `ContractPendingVersionRoundMismatchError` (the
     pending version changed since the client looked: replaced, or cancelled and reopened).
  4. Authority. `Cancel`: `self.owner_id() == contract.owner_id()`. `TopUp`: the contract owner
     **or** the recorded payer (`round.payer().identity_id() == Some(self.owner_id())`).
     Otherwise `ContractPendingVersionActionNotAllowedError`.
  5. `TopUp` on a `Crossed` round is `ContractPendingVersionAlreadyCrossedError`: the
     verification walk is finished, the round is in its wait, and nothing remains to fund (the
     fund would only be refunded at activation). `Cancel` is allowed in both states (Q13:
     "including during the wait").
  6. `Ok(ContractPendingVersionTransitionAction::from_borrowed_transition_with_round(self, round).into())`.
- `has_state_validation` true; `validates_full_state_on_check_tx` false (default); address
  traits: the "no addresses" groups, as the fee claim.

Ordering with the block event (A11): the readiness event runs inside `run_dao_platform_events`,
before this block's transitions. A round activated (R08-13) or retired by the event of block `H`
is gone when `H`'s transitions run, so a cancellation in `H` is refused as
`ContractPendingVersionNotFoundError` and paid: "a transaction processed after a boundary cannot
retroactively cancel activation at that boundary". A cancellation committed in `H - 1` or earlier
removed the deadline entry with the round, so the event of `H` finds nothing due. Two
cancellations of one contract in one block: transitions execute sequentially against the block's
transaction, so the second sees no round and is refused. All three are tests (section 6).

### 3.4 Replacement (Q12)

Delivered as: the primitive (R08-12 part 1), the invariants pinned here through a block, and the
converter contract for R06-04. What the tests pin (section 6, "Replacement"): a replacement is a
single batch; the old round is retired (pointer names the new id, old record and count tree
unreachable, retired queue holds the old id, old deadline entry and emptied bucket gone, old fund
deleted, cleanup reserve to the pool, remainder to the **old** payer, netted into one write when
the payers match); the new round is `Pending` with raw count zero, no cursor and no mark; the next
block's event judges the new round and cannot cross it on the old reports; a report inserted
under the old round id changes nothing for the new round; a stale deadline never activates; the
activation record of the currently active version (R08-13) is untouched; and the cleanup step
drains the old round in later blocks. Exactly one pending update per contract is structural: the
pointer holds one round id and the primitive replaces rather than refuses. R06-04's converter
therefore needs no "is there a pending version" check for updates that carry code; it emits
`ReadinessOperationType::OpenRound` with `ReadinessRoundFunding { initial_funding:
fee.dashvm_readiness.readiness_fund_required_amount, cleanup_reserve: fee.dashvm_readiness.round_cleanup_reserve }`
(the row this task appends, 3.5) and the payer it records (`ReadinessPayer::Identity(owner)` until
FIX-08 adds the contract bucket). Sent as a note (section 11).

### 3.5 Refunds, top-ups and the fee row (Q13)

Refund rules, all inside the R08-12 primitive and pinned here at the transition level:
`refund = fund_balance - min(round_cleanup_reserve, fund_balance)`, credited to
`round.payer()`; the reserve is charged to the epoch processing pool of the block that retires the
round (it pays the deferred cleanup); a payer who funded nothing beyond the reserve receives zero
and no balance write happens. A top-up by the owner when the payer is someone else raises the
fund, and the remainder still returns to the recorded payer (Q13's "recorded payer"; the owner
chose to fund a round somebody else paid for). A top-up on a round that never becomes fund-short
is returned in full, minus nothing, at retirement.

Fee row: `FeeDashVmReadinessVersion.readiness_fund_required_amount: Credits = 20_000_000`
(provisional; the value the R08-12 Drive tests fund rounds with; 500 complete walks of 400
evonodes at 100 credits per lookup, or 20 restarts of a 1,000-evonode walk) appended to R08-12
part 2's struct in `fee/dashvm_readiness/v1.rs`, doc comment naming its reader (the contract
create/update converter, R06-04) and the reserve it must exceed. This task's drive-abci test
helper opens rounds with it (so the row is exercised) and R08-12's "the group has exactly the rows
it declares" test is extended. No minimum on a top-up beyond `amount > 0`: a top-up smaller than
one page's fee leaves a fund-short round short, which only the payer pays for, and the money
returns at retirement.

### 3.6 No automatic expiry and recovery without a below-threshold fallback

Nothing to implement in consensus beyond what R08-12 leaves out on purpose (no deadline for
collecting votes, no time-based crossing, no fund-exhaustion retirement). This task pins the
absences with tests against part 2's event (section 6, "No expiry"): a pending round with zero
reports, and one at four of six evonodes, are visited for 1,000 blocks spanning ten years of block
time and stay `Pending` with their fund intact and no refund; a fund-short round waits for as long
as nobody tops it up; a node reopened from its tempdir between blocks holds the same round, mark,
cursor and fund; a cancellation after the long wait refunds the full remainder. The node-local
half is named in the PR body: the compiled cache, the "locally unready" state and preparation
from the activation record after restart, cache loss or engine upgrade are R08-06's (and FIX-09's
`NodeFault` classification); readiness is an availability mechanism, and a node that is unready
never rejects a consensus-valid call and never charges a cold-cache fee (Q14).

## 5. Provisional values and interpretations (the PR body repeats them)

1. `StateTransitionType::ContractPendingVersion = 26`; FIX-09's report takes 27 (A02, register).
2. State codes 41300..41303 in a new compilation readiness sub-band; the basic amount error takes
   the next free `106xx` code.
3. `readiness_fund_required_amount = 20_000_000` credits (A15, unbenchmarked), read by the opening
   path; no top-up minimum beyond a positive amount.
4. Authority: the contract owner cancels; the contract owner or the recorded payer tops up; the
   refund always goes to the recorded payer whoever topped up. Declared update authority (R11-14,
   contract groups) replaces the owner rule in a later state generation.
5. The transition binds the pending round id; a stale id is a paid refusal, never a cancel of
   whatever is current.
6. A top-up of a crossed round is refused; a cancellation is accepted in both states.
7. A top-up does not touch the round record; the walk's own `awaiting_funding` flag and unwritten
   mark make it resume.
8. Replacement is triggered by the contract update that carries code (R06-04), not by a transition
   here; the primitive and the invariants are pinned here through a block.
9. Proof of execution is the round's fund item (absent after cancellation, rewritten after a
   top-up); the result variant carries contract, round and the fund's post-state.
10. The min fee floor reuses `credit_transfer`; the round id travels as `Bytes32`.
11. Refusals are paid (bump of the contract nonce), as for every identity-signed contract
    transition; nothing here is an unpaid rejection.

**rs-drive** (`drive/votes/readiness/tests.rs`): `fetch_readiness_round_with_fee` returns the
round and a non-zero fee, `None` for a contract without one; a cancellation batch and a
replacement batch touch only paths under `[112, r]`, `[40, 129]`, the identity balance tree and

**rs-drive-abci pipeline** (`contract_pending_version/tests.rs`; `Setup` with `Actor`s owner,

  unreachable; retired queue holds the round; fund item gone; `round_cleanup_reserve` in the epoch
  processing pool; `funding - reserve` on the payer (who is not the owner); owner charged the fee;
  contract nonce bumped; `calculate_total_credits_balance` balances.
- Cancel during the wait: a crossed round with a deadline; success; deadline entry and its emptied
  bucket gone; `fetch_readiness_rounds_due(deadline + 1 hour)` is empty; refund as above.
- Cancel refusals, each paid, each bumping the nonce, each leaving the round untouched: by the
  payer who is not the owner and by a stranger (41302); with a stale round id (41301); with no
  current round (41300); on an unknown contract (10400); twice in one block (the second 41300);
  after the round retired earlier in the same block through `activate_readiness_round_operations`
  applied in the block's transaction before the transitions (41300: the boundary rule).
- Top-up by the payer: fund grows by `amount`, payer debited `amount + fee`; by the owner: owner
  debited, fund grows, and a later cancellation refunds the whole remainder to the **payer**; by a
  stranger: 41302; `amount: 0`: the basic code, refused in `check_tx` (`FirstTimeCheck`) and in the
  block; insufficient balance for `amount + fee`: `IdentityInsufficientBalanceError` at the
  pre-check; on a crossed round: 41303; conservation after every accepted top-up.
- `check_tx` accepts a well-formed cancel and top-up (no state validation there) and the block
  then refuses the stale-id one, proving mempool admission is not a promise.
- Proofs: `prove_state_transition` and `verify_state_transition_was_executed_with_proof` after a
  cancellation (fund absent, result `None`), after a top-up (result `Some(fund)`), and a proof for

- Gate: at `PlatformVersion::get(16)` on a genesis-16 platform the transition is refused with
  `StateTransitionNotActiveError`; at latest it executes.
- Fee schedule: the readiness group has exactly three rows (R08-12's test extended).
